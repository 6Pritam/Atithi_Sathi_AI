## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a refined intersection between time-honored Indian hospitality (*Atithi Devo Bhava*) and intelligent, contemporary conversational AI. It speaks to discerning luxury travelers, business executives, and boutique hotel guests who expect immediate, intuitive assistance without friction or visual noise.

The visual direction embraces a **Warm Tactile Minimalism** informed by authentic Indian materiality—sandstone, unbleached linen, cardamom leaf, and hand-ground terracotta. Unlike cold, clinical AI platforms, this system breathes quiet luxury: generous negative space, gentle architectural layering, nuanced borders, and a balanced typographic rhythm that balances modern geometric precision with organic grace. Interactions evoke the effortless serenity of a 5-star concierge lobby: prompt, grounded, discreet, and deeply welcoming.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from organic, architectural pigments to create an inviting environment suitable for round-the-clock use.

- **Primary Accent (`#C85A32` / `#B85028`)**: Deep Indian terracotta saffron. Used strictly for high-intent actions—booking confirms, primary call-to-actions, active state toggles, and brand moments. It must never overpower the tranquil canvas.
- **Secondary Accent (`#2D5A43` / `#3A6351`)**: Earthy cardamom forest green. Represents reassurance, verified hotel services, high guest ratings, sustainability badges, and affirmative feedback.
- **Canvas & Structural Surfaces**:
  - `Base Background`: `#FAF9F6` (Warm Alabaster / Ivory) forms a soft, non-glare foundation.
  - `Secondary Background`: `#F3F1EC` (Soft Sand / Warm Linen) demarcates chat feeds, system backdrops, and side rails.
  - `Elevated Surface`: `#FFFFFF` for floating cards, modals, and response modules.
  - `Interactive / Hover`: `#ECE8DF` for button hover, focused list items, and selected chips.
- **Line & Border**: `#E6E2D8` (structural) and `#E8E5DE` (cards) act as faint boundaries, eliminating the need for hard divisions.
- **Typography Tones**: `#1C1C1C` delivers deep charcoal clarity for headings and primary content, while `#6B6B6B` provides balanced, legible hierarchy for metadata, timestamps, and assistive guidance.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes high-legibility hospitality interactions using **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Its sculpted geometric curves bring approachable modern clarity, while its tall x-height and open counters maintain effortless scanning across dense itinerary schedules and multi-turn concierge conversations.

- **Scale & Rhythm**: Headings set the conversational tone with slight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to ground luxury stature without display theatrics.
- **Reading Comfort**: Body copy adheres strictly to a relaxed `1.6` line-height ratio (`15px` on `24px`, `16px` on `26px`), accommodating extended reading of dining itineraries, cultural notes, and stay summaries.
- **Micro-Copy**: Labels, tags, and category kickers use medium-to-semibold weights with subtle letter tracking (`0.01em` to `0.04em`) to maintain sharp legibility when rendered in caps or compact badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy balances a focused conversational feed with modular concierge sidebars. Content lives within structured, soft grids that prevent cognitive overload.

- **Grid Architecture**:
  - **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column grid with a structured split layout. The AI conversational thread occupies a dedicated central column (max width 768px), bordered by persistent contextual panels (stay itineraries, room service cart, quick prompts) spanning remaining columns. Outer margins are `2.5rem` (`40px`), gutters `1.5rem` (`24px`).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with sheet overlays or bottom draw-ups for collateral widgets. Margins scale to `1.5rem` (`24px`).
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: Fluid single-column architecture, pinned bottom composer, edge margins of `1rem` (`16px`), and gutters of `1rem` (`16px`).
- **Vertical Spacing Cadence**:
  - Elements within chat bubbles and list cards use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px).
  - Component inner paddings standardise on `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px).
  - Distance between discrete conversational turns or distinct widget clusters adheres to `space-xl` (32px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **ambient hospitality layering**—soft, cloud-like shadows anchored by hairline borders, rather than dramatic drop-shadows or stark borders.

- **Base Layer (Flat)**: The `#FAF9F6` base sits at ground level with no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Block)**:
  - `box-shadow`: `0 2px 10px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.04)`
  - `border`: `1px solid #E8E5DE`
  - Applied to assistant response cards, room amenity cards, and recommendation blocks.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action Bar & Input Composer)**:
  - `box-shadow`: `0 8px 30px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.06)`
  - `border`: `1px solid #E6E2D8`
  - Applied to the bottom pinned chat input, active dropdowns, and flyout filters.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Full Sheets)**:
  - `box-shadow`: `0 16px 40px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.10)`
  - Paired with an alabaster backdrop blur overlay (`rgba(250, 249, 246, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).

## Shapes

The shape system employs generous, organic curvature that mirrors handcrafted clay and rounded stonework:

- **Cards & Service Modules**: `16px` (`rounded-2xl` / `1rem`) for approachable containment without appearing childlike.
- **Inputs, Chips & Secondary Buttons**: `12px` (`rounded-xl` / `0.75rem`) for natural finger and eye tracking.
- **Interactive Pills, Avatars & Composer Capsules**: Fully rounded (`9999px` / `rounded-full`) for quick-reply prompts, status badges, and floating command inputs.
- **Inner Corner Rule**: Nested elements maintain concentric curvature—an inner element nested within a `16px` container uses `8px` or `12px` to prevent visual awkwardness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#C85A32`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded `12px` (or `rounded-full` for compact CTAs). Hover state shifts to `#B85028`. Active state scales to `0.98`. Focus ring: `2px offset` with `#C85A32`.
- **Secondary / Soft**: Background `#ECE8DF`, text `#1C1C1C`, border `1px solid #E6E2D8`. Hover state `#E2DDD2`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent background, text `#1C1C1C`, hover background `#F3F1EC`.

### Chips & Quick-Replies
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E6E2D8`, text `#1C1C1C`, border-radius `rounded-full`.
- Padding: `8px 16px`.
- Hover state: Background `#ECE8DF`, border-color `#C85A32` at `20%` opacity.
- Selected state: Background `#2D5A43`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-color `#2D5A43`.

### Input Fields & Search
- Field height: `48px`. Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E6E2D8`, border-radius `12px`.
- Typography: `body-md` in `#1C1C1C`, placeholder in `#6B6B6B`.
- Focus state: Border `#C85A32`, glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.12)`.

### Pinned AI Composer
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), white elevated card with ambient shadow (`0 8px 30px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.06)`), border `1px solid #E6E2D8`.
- Integrated microphone and submit buttons use circular icons with terracotta fill on focus.

### Cards & Itinerary Modules
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E5DE`, border-radius `16px`, diffuse shadow `0 2px 10px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.04)`.
- Internal sections are separated by hairline dividers (`#F3F1EC`), padding `16px` or `20px`.

### Badges & Verification Tags
- **Hotel Verified / Eco-Conscious / High Rating**: Cardamom green tint surface (`rgba(45, 90, 67, 0.08)`), text `#2D5A43`, border `1px solid rgba(45, 90, 67, 0.2)`. Border-radius `rounded-full`.
- **Alert / Essential Note**: Terracotta tint surface (`rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.08)`), text `#C85A32`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Size: `20px × 20px`. Unchecked: border `1.5px solid #E6E2D8`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: Background `#2D5A43`, checkmark `#FFFFFF`, border-color `#2D5A43`. Radio follows identical behavior with a centered dot.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Transparent list items with `#FAF9F6` alternating rows or faint divider lines (`#E6E2D8`).
- Active items highlight in `#ECE8DF` with a subtle `3px` left-edge indicator in `#C85A32`.
